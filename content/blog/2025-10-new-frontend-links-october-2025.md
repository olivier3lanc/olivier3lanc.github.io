## Tools

* [Tooooools](https://www.tooooools.app/) Tooooools (exactly 6 o's), free minimalist and lo-fi effects for image and video processing online.
* [CrawlerCheck](https://crawlercheck.com/) Instantly check if Google bots and AI crawlers can access your website.
* [PicTiler](https://pictiler.com/) Transform Your Photos Into Mosaics
* [MediaBunny](https://github.com/Vanilagy/mediabunny) Pure TypeScript media toolkit for reading, writing, and converting video and audio files, directly in the browser
* [Lossless Cut](https://github.com/mifi/lossless-cut) The swiss army knife of lossless video/audio editing 
* [ASCII Flow](https://asciiflow.com/) Infinite ASCII diagrams, save to Google Drive, resize, freeform draw, and export straight to text/html.

## JavaScript

* [Dual Range Input](https://github.com/stanko/dual-range-input) Native dual range input in 60 lines of JavaScript. 
* [Range Slider Element](https://github.com/andreruffert/range-slider-element) 🎚 A cross browser customizable and accessible `<range-slider>` web component
* [Peak.js](https://codeberg.org/chrisn/peaks.js) JavaScript UI component for interacting with audio waveforms
* [Shiki.js](https://github.com/shikijs/shiki) A beautiful yet powerful syntax highlighter
